Where is Pasta?

Pasta lives in the menu bar at the top right of your screen. It has no Dock icon. Click the bowl icon, or press Shift-Command-V, to open your history.

How do I paste an item?

Open Pasta, find the item (type to search), then click it or press Return. Pasta copies it and takes you back to your app, so just press Command-V. Turn on "Paste directly after picking" in Settings to skip that step.

Can I change the shortcuts?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear icon, or Command-comma while Pasta is open), go to Shortcuts, click a shortcut and press the new keys.

Why isn't something I copied in the list?

Pasta saves text only. It skips passwords that password managers mark as concealed, and nothing is saved while saving is paused (the menu bar icon looks faded).

How do I start Pasta automatically?

Settings → General → turn on "Launch Pasta at login".

How do I quit Pasta?

Right-click the menu bar icon and choose Quit Pasta.

Is my clipboard history private?

Yes. It never leaves your Mac. See the privacy policy.
