Pasta keeps everything you copy in your Mac's menu bar, ready to search and paste again. Press Shift-Command-V from any app, type a few letters, press Return, and paste.

  • Saves your text clipboard history automatically
  • Instant search with highlighted matches
  • Pin favourites, filter by type, sort by last used or date created
  • Every keyboard shortcut can be changed
  • Colorful or Classic menu bar icon
  • Private: your history never leaves your Mac
Pasta = paste + fun.
